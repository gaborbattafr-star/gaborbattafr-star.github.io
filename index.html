A Tortenet

Brutalis tamadas aldozatat 6 evig figyelmen kivul hagyta a francia rendorseg — most borton fenyegeti

2019 augusztusaban Batta Gabort harom ismeretlen elköveto brutálisan megtamadta Strasbourg Parc de la Citadelle parkjaban. Sulyos arccsonttoreseket szenvedett, surgos mutetre szorult a Hopital de Hautepierre korhazban.

A francia hatasagok közel hat evig erdemi nyomozast nem folytattak. A tettesek maig azonositatlanok es szabadlabon vannak.

Eközben PKKB 16.B.21.716/2018 ugy alapjan 42 napos elzarast rendeltek el ellene — hatarido: 2026. aprilis 29.

The Story

Violent attack victim ignored by French police for 6 years — now facing imprisonment

In August 2019, Gabor Batta was brutally attacked in Strasbourg's Parc de la Citadelle. He suffered severe facial fractures requiring emergency surgery at Hopital de Hautepierre.

Despite filing a formal complaint, French authorities conducted no meaningful investigation for nearly six years. The attackers remain unidentified and at large.

Meanwhile, case PKKB 16.B.21.716/2018 resulted in a 42-day imprisonment order — deadline: April 29, 2026.
